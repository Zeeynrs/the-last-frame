## Objective
Write one beat sheet for "The Forgotten Station" that a puzzle designer (TLF-004/005) and an implementer can both build from.

## Context
- `docs/GDD.md` §1, §4, §5, §8–9, §12, §15

- `docs/ADR_LOG.md`: **SD-1** (Ch1 mechanic scope) and **ADR-014** (Echo vs Trace naming)

Allowed Ch1 mechanics: Rewind, Freeze, Echo (record/replay, 1 at a time), Trace (reveal residual traces), one doorway-reassignment spatial foreshadow. Fast-Forward is optional. Time Shift, room rotation, gravity, folding and temporal energy are **out**.

## Forbidden files

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] 8–14 ordered beats. Each lists: location, player objective, mechanic taught or tested, narrative information revealed, emotional tone, estimated minutes.
- [ ] Total estimate is 30–45 min. At least 5 beats are marked as puzzle slots (`CH1-PZ-0N` + one-line intent; full specs belong to TLF-004).
- [ ] Includes: pocket-watch tutorial, first Echo puzzle, the spatial foreshadow beat, at least one NPC or recorded character presence, exactly one major revelation, one cinematic beat, an exit hook into Chapter 2.
- [ ] Canon unchanged (Elias, Mira, Operator = future Elias, Archivist, Elias-0, city frozen at 23:59, the loop, 4 endings). Anything canon-adjacent goes in an **Open canon questions** section, not into the beats.
- [ ] Section **Naming proposal (ADR-014)**: 2–3 names for the reveal ability (working name "Trace"), with rationale.
- [ ] Section **Mechanics outside SD-1 I wanted**: a list, or "none".

## Tests
Self-check against each criterion above. Opus reviews against GDD canon and SD-1.

## Deliverables
The file committed on the task branch and pushed. Handoff report: summary, files changed, open questions.

## Stop conditions
Stop and report if a canon contradiction is needed, a beat only works with an out-of-scope mechanic, or any forbidden path would need editing.
