# Task: TLF-084 — Shadows and depth (ADR-027 proposal + implementation in the ADR-026 pass)

**Owner:** Opus (technical) · **Visual review:** Sol (stand-in Fable 5.2 when Sol is offline, owner-approved) · **Lead:** decision, validate, merge · **Priority:** P1 · **Status:** READY

## Goal
The owner (2026-11-11): from now on add lighting **and shadow** so every room has atmosphere and depth. ADR-026 (palette-indexed ramp shift) lights rooms but casts no shadows; objects float on the floor and the floor reads flat. Add shadows and occlusion **inside the same palette-safe model** (still 32 colours, 0 off-palette pixels, one setting turns it all off), in iso and side view.

## Part 1 — options and recommendation (`docs/reviews/TLF-084_shadow_options.md`)
Compare at least:
1. **Pass-side darkening shapes** (extra ramp steps, `k = clamp(A - lift + shade, 0, 3)` or similar):
   - **Contact shadows** under every grounded body (Elias, NPC statues, freestanding props and consoles), sized from the footprint/collider, 2:1 in iso.
   - **Cast shadows** from tall freestanding objects (pillars, statues, consoles, gate posts) away from the room's key light (the focal `RoomLight`), as a floor polygon whose length follows the object height; static objects baked once per room, Elias contact-only (or a short dynamic cast).
   - **Ambient occlusion strips** on the floor along the base of N/W walls and low partitions, and in corners.
2. **Authored shadow sprites** (art from Sol/Argon, palette 00/01, no alpha blending) placed under objects.
3. Any better option you find (e.g. a coarse shadow mask texture per room generated by the builder).

For each: palette fit, how a room author adds it (ideally automatic from existing colliders/footprints plus a few overrides), readability (shadows never hide clues, targets, Elias or information regions; info regions and exempt indices ≥ 25 stay untouched), interaction with lights (does a light cancel a shadow, ordering rules), side view (ADR-025), cost on the low-end target, test impact, the setting (`lighting_enabled` off = today's unlit look, byte-identical).

Include a spike on the lit Concourse West (scratch copy only): arrival, gate, dials, east door, lit / lit+shadows / unlit, greyscale copies.
