# TLF-085: `concrete_service` floor tile moved into the floor ramp (ADR-027 receivers)

**Branch** `agent/opus-core-concrete-floor`, from `origin/main` `f7649fd`. It is one commit, made before the Maintenance Bay lighting. Not merged. Art review: Fable/Sol.

## Why

- **The decision:** Lead, 2026-11-12, option (a).
- **The gap:** ADR-027 floor fill and shade apply only to the floor ramp, indices 00–05. `ch01_env_tile_concrete_service_idle` was drawn entirely in the steel ramp, indices 11/12/13. So the fill and shadows reached only these shares of each room's floor pixels:

| Room | Floor pixels reached |
|---|---|
| Maintenance Bay | about 4% |
| Security Crossing | 5% |
| Signal Control | 7% |

  Every terrazzo room is at about 90–100%.
- **Why not the alternatives:** option (b) would have widened the receivers to 11–13, which shades steel prop bases; option (c), lighting these rooms without floor fill or shadows, is ruled out by the owner.

## What changed

- **`tools/art/gen/tiles.py` `concrete()`:** the generator is the tile's only source, so no remap script was needed. Each steel index maps to the floor-ramp step of nearest Rec.709 luminance, the measure the greyscale proofs use (`CONCRETE_STEEL_TO_FLOOR`):

| Old index | Luminance | New index | Luminance |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| 11 | 29.4 | **01** | 25.6 |
| 12 | 50.8 | **03** | 56.0 |
| 13 | 77.9 | **04** | 78.6 |

  The pattern, joints, stencil and rim are unchanged, and the three value levels keep their order.
- **The tile itself:** `assets/source/ch01/ch01_env_tile_concrete_service_idle.pxo` was rewritten by `gen_all.py --only concrete_service --export`, and the `.png` is the Pixelorama export.
  - **Pixel mapping:** exact. 58 px went 11→01, 197 px 12→03 and 33 px 13→04; alpha is identical.
- **Generated mockups that embed the tile**, regenerated by `gen_all.py --sheet` (every other mockup came out byte-identical):
  - `design/art/mockups/ch01_security_crossing_mockup.png`
  - `design/art/mockups/ch01_signal_control_mockup.png`
  - `design/art/mockups/ch01_step1_props_greyscale_proof.png`
- **`design/art/ASSET_METADATA.md`:** the tile's palette line now reads `01,03,04`.
- **No other asset changes.**

**Checks** (`logs/`):
- `verify_regen.py`: ALL REPRODUCIBLE.
- `verify_regen.py --pixelorama` on the tile: `.pxo` byte-identical, PNG pixel-identical, and a fresh Pixelorama re-export byte-identical.
  - The full `--pixelorama` sweep is slower than the time I allowed and was cut off. Before the cut, one unrelated asset, `ch01_ui_wheel_seg2_selected`, showed a differing Pixelorama re-export. That file is untouched here, and its `.pxo` and pixels are identical.
- `check_assets.py`: ALL PASS (314 files).
