## Findings and required fixes

### R1 — Blocking: floor plates and doorway family break the room projection

`tools/art/gen/step1_props.py:763` draws the shared floor plate as an axis-aligned screen rectangle (`rect(2,2,29,13)`). All eight A/B, alarm and standby states inherit this. They read as frontal control badges, not surfaces lying on the 2:1 floor. The glyph/state distinctions work, but the surface geometry does not match the existing pressure switch or floor diamonds.

`tools/art/gen/step1_props.py:255` similarly gives all five doorway families a horizontal lintel and level jamb feet. The platform gate in `ch01_step1_room_statues_mockup.png` visibly stands frontal across an isometric room edge rather than aligning with the east archway. Open/closed silhouettes are legible; their orientation still prevents a coherent 1:1 art swap.

**Fix:** draw the floor plates on the 2:1 floor plane, with consistent recessed/raised profiles and readable glyphs. Align gate/staff door/hatch/inner shutter/barrier headers, thresholds and shutter geometry with the relevant existing archway axis. Keep top-left lighting and the approved state symbols. Coordinate canvas, pivot and axis conventions with Opus if a technical exception is necessary; do not solve this by distorting textures at runtime. Add colour/greyscale in-room proofs showing a plate on floor diamonds and a closed/open gate in the actual archway. Also show the selectors as wall-mounted fixtures: the present room proof places them free-standing on the floor (`step1_mockups.py:74`).

### R2 — Blocking: assembled commuter proofs cannot be reproduced from the delivered PNG components

`tools/art/gen/step1_statues.py:741` merges unoutlined body/overlay interiors and then computes a new outer outline around the combined silhouette. The exported assets instead contain outlined base bodies and separately outlined idle overlays (`:785` and the overlay registry). No combined read silhouettes or matching read overlay/mask assets are delivered. Compositing the supplied PNGs therefore does not reproduce the proof, particularly the static outline around trolley, crank and bags.

Independent comparison of the exported base PNG followed by its declared overlay PNGs, against `assemble_commuter`, found differences for every commuter:

**Fix:** retain the modular body/overlay sources, and supply an exportable assembly/read-outline contract that produces the approved combined silhouette using delivered assets. A straightforward option is registered, reproducible assembled idle/read exports for the thirteen named commuters in addition to the reusable parts. Agree the import contract with Opus/Flash. Rebuild the proof from the actual runtime PNGs, with no proof-only re-outlining step. The static reduced-effects silhouette must cover the complete person and held prop. Supply source/export/metadata entries for any added assets and update the exact Pixelorama count.

### R3 — Required handoff corrections: metadata does not match the proof or established layers

`design/art/ASSET_METADATA.md:784` lists the all-thirteen proof as 1152×936 and prop proof as 1320×1224. The actual PNGs are **1320×1272** and **1320×1580**. These prose-only entries are not caught by the metadata table size checker.

The new metadata assigns all fixtures/portals/bulkhead to `YSort_Props (z_index = 10)` and floor plates to `z_index = 1`. This disagrees with the Bible's named layers/conventions (wall fixtures 7, floor-level props 8, free-standing props in `YSort_Actors` 10, wall modules 5). Have Opus confirm the intended per-asset mapping; record the correct placement-specific layers instead of giving the whole batch one generic assignment. Also reconcile the terminal handoff's dead-relay 20×36 claim with the exported/metadata 24×36 canvas.
