The structure is strong. The teaching curve (Trace → Rewind → Freeze → Echo → combination → doorway graph) is clean and every slot ties to an ADR.

### Required changes
| # | Severity | Finding | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | **Major (canon pacing)** | Beat 09 delivers **two** major revelations: Elias's clearance froze the station **and** an explicit loop with 47 prior exits and memory wipes. The loop is the game's core late twist (GDD §1 "has happened before", Ch5 "repeated journeys"); stating it outright in Ch1 front-loads it and breaks "exactly one revelation". | Keep **self-implication** as the single revelation. Turn the loop into an *ambiguous seed*: the audit log shows an unexplained scrolling list of `23:59` entries under user `ELIAS`, with no count, no "exit" label and no memory-wipe statement. The Operator's Beat 10 line should no longer confirm it. |
| D2 | Minor | Beat 10 introduces an "earpiece" for the Operator, but GDD §4 says he speaks "through old communication devices" | Use a plaza payphone, a broadcast speaker or the civic screen's audio |
| D3 | Minor | Puzzle IDs use dual notation `CH1-PZ-01 (CH1-PZ-001)` | Use `CH1-PZ-001…005` only (matches PUZZLE_DESIGN.md) |

### Answers to open canon questions
1. **Loop counter:** no fixed number in Ch1. An unspecified scrolling list of `23:59` timestamps (see D1).
2. **Operator phrasing:** allowed but subtle. At most one or two phrases shared between Elias and the Operator across Ch1, and nothing explicit.
3. **Archivist foreshadowing:** defer to Ch2/Ch3. Ch1 stays focused.

### Notes forwarded to TLF-004/005/010 (not blocking)
- Beat 07 (CH1-PZ-004) combines Echo, Rewind and Freeze at once as the 4th puzzle, which is a difficulty spike. It needs a strong hint ladder and could be split into two steps.
- Beat 04: the falling shutter must never harm or trap Elias. Make it non-lethal and resettable.
- Beat 06: Mira's line "let the clock pass midnight" is good foreshadowing, but it mustn't read as the "correct" ending (GDD §12: endings are earned, not prescribed).
- Art scope: 9 distinct rooms. TLF-005/010 should plan a modular station kit so the slice stays producible.
