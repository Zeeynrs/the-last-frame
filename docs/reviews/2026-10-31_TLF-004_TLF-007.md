### Contract gaps resolved by the Lead (see ADR_LOG)
The specs rely on behavior the ADRs didn't define. Decisions taken today:
- **ADR-015 (new), pocket-watch UX and targeting.** How an ability picks its target (PZ-002 has the shutter and gear side by side; PZ-001 has gate and pillar), and how Echo record/stop/play/reset is triggered (the input map had no reset action).
- **ADR-007 amendment 2, history commit semantics.** Per-tick counters (`hold_ticks`, `decay_ticks`, `engage_ticks`) would have flooded the 32-entry history buffer and made Rewind step back one tick. Now only discrete transitions commit, and the cursor/branch rule is defined. Verified against TEST-PZ001-03/04 and TEST-PZ002-01/05.
- **ADR-007 amendment 2, Freeze toggles.** A second Freeze on a frozen object unfreezes it (PZ-002/004 recovery paths depend on this).
- **ADR-008 amendment, echo scope.** Echoes don't replay pocket-watch abilities. Triggers occupied when recording starts are seeded at `rel_tick 0` (PZ-003 alternative). A room change stops the recording.
- **ADR-006 clarification.** Triggers don't return reasons; each trigger handler decides per actor (completion triggers ignore echoes).
- **ADR-009 clarification.** Doorway IDs are room-local keys `(room_id, door_id)`, not IdRegistry IDs. Physically blocked pairings (the niche grille) are valid edges that don't count toward reachability.
- **ADR-016 (new), puzzle completion and reset policy.**

### Notes for implementation (TLF-008/009, not blocking)
- PZ-002's 180-tick window assumes ADR-015 targeting (Freeze auto-prefers the gear). Playtest it; the assist setting raises it to 300.
- The "Accessibility Assist: extended timing windows" setting that PZ-002 introduces joins the FR-018 settings list.
