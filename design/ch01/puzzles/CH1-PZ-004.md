# Puzzle Specification: CH1-PZ-004 — The Synchronized Substation

## 1. Standard Puzzle Specification (`PUZZLE_DESIGN.md` §2)

- **Puzzle ID:** `CH1-PZ-004`
- **Name:** The Synchronized Substation
- **Chapter / Room:** Chapter 1 (*The Forgotten Station*) / Signal Control (`ch01.signal_control`, Beat 07)
- **Narrative purpose:** Test the player's mastery of `Rewind`, `Freeze`, and `Echo` in a fair **two-step sequence** to restore auxiliary power to the Upper Vestibule and Station Atrium. Booting the Signal Control monitors reveals that Elysium-09 is enclosed in an artificial temporal boundary anchored to the Atrium above, prompting The Operator's urgent intercom warning not to inspect the Atrium dispatch logs.
- **Player objective:**
  - **Step 1 (Power Bus Stabilization):** Restore charge to the central surge capacitor (`ch01.signal_control.surge_capacitor`) with `Rewind` and lock its charge with `Freeze` so the main breaker bus stays powered.
  - **Step 2 (Breaker Synchronization):** Record an `Echo` pulling Primary Breaker A (`ch01.signal_control.breaker_a`), walk to Secondary Breaker B (`ch01.signal_control.breaker_b`), replay the `Echo`, and pull Breaker B within the 180-tick (3.0 s) synchronization window to unlock `ch01.signal_control.vestibule_door`.
- **Required abilities:** `Rewind`, `Freeze`, `Echo`
- **Required objects:**
  - `ch01.signal_control.surge_capacitor` (Central glass-coil capacitor bank with `REWIND | FREEZE` capabilities)
  - `ch01.signal_control.breaker_a` (Primary Breaker A on the west mezzanine, `Interactable`)
  - `ch01.signal_control.breaker_b` (Secondary Breaker B on the east mezzanine, `Interactable`)
  - `ch01.signal_control.substation_grid` (Substation logic controller / monitor bank tracking Step 1 & Step 2 state)
  - `ch01.signal_control.vestibule_door` (Powered bulkhead door leading to `ch01.upper_vestibule`)
  - `ch01.signal_control.reset_anchor_01` (Substation master reset switch at the center console)
  - `ch01.signal_control.exit_trigger_vestibule` (`Area2D` completion trigger beyond `vestibule_door`)
- **Initial room state:**
  - `ch01.signal_control.surge_capacitor` is at the center of the room in `charge_state = &"discharged"`, `decay_ticks = 0`, `bus_powered = false`, `is_frozen = false`.
  - Floor power conduits leading from `surge_capacitor` to `breaker_a` (west) and `breaker_b` (east) are dark.
  - `ch01.signal_control.breaker_a` and `ch01.signal_control.breaker_b` are in `lever_state = &"open"`, `engage_ticks = 0`. Attempting to pull either breaker while `bus_powered == false` returns `&"unpowered"`.
  - `ch01.signal_control.vestibule_door` is `door_state = &"locked"`, `passage_blocked = true`.
- **Rules the player must discover:**
  1. **Decoupled Two-Step Structure:** Breakers A and B cannot be engaged while the central `surge_capacitor` is discharged (`&"unpowered"`). Stabilizing the capacitor (`Rewind` → `Freeze`) permanently powers the bus (`bus_powered = true`) until the player manually unfreezes or resets it, so the player **never** has to juggle capacitor decay and breaker timing simultaneously.
  2. `surge_capacitor` supports both `REWIND` and `FREEZE` on the same object: `Rewind` restores its pre-arrival `"charged"` state (`decay_ticks = 240` = 4.0 s), and `Freeze` halts the decay countdown, locking `bus_powered = true`.
  3. Once `bus_powered == true`, pulling `breaker_a` or `breaker_b` (`&"use"`) engages its spring-loaded magnetic contact for `180` ticks (3.0 s). Because walking between the west and east mezzanines takes ~`360` ticks (6.0 s), one person cannot hold both breakers engaged at the same time.
  4. Both `breaker_a` and `breaker_b` are high-voltage chrono-shielded switches (`capabilities == NONE`), so they must be coordinated using an `Echo` (`&"echo.0"`).
- **Step-by-step intended solution:**
  - **Step 1 — Stabilize the Capacitor Bus:**
    1. Target `ch01.signal_control.surge_capacitor` with `Rewind` (`&"rewind"`): it restores `charge_state = &"charged"`, `decay_ticks = 240`, `bus_powered = true`.
    2. Within `240` ticks (4.0 s), switch to `Freeze` and activate `&"freeze"` on `ch01.signal_control.surge_capacitor`.
    3. `surge_capacitor.is_frozen` becomes `true`, locking `bus_powered = true`. The west and east floor conduits light up with steady cyan chevron strips, confirming Step 1 is complete.
  - **Step 2 — Synchronize the Breakers with Echo:**
    4. Walk to `ch01.signal_control.breaker_a` on the west mezzanine and start `Echo` recording (`rel_tick = 0`).
    5. Interact (`&"use"`) with `ch01.signal_control.breaker_a` (`rel_tick = 15..60`), then stop `Echo` recording (`rel_tick = 30..90`).
    6. Walk across the mezzanine to `ch01.signal_control.breaker_b` on the east side.
    7. Start `Echo` playback: `&"echo.0"` spawns at `breaker_a` and pulls it at `rel_tick = 15..60`, opening a 180-tick (3.0 s) synchronization window on `breaker_a`.
    8. Pull `ch01.signal_control.breaker_b` (`&"use"`) while `breaker_a.lever_state == &"engaged"` (or up to 179 ticks before `&"echo.0"` pulls `breaker_a`).
    9. With `bus_powered == true`, `breaker_a.lever_state == &"engaged"`, and `breaker_b.lever_state == &"engaged"` on the same tick, `substation_grid.grid_online` locks to `true`, unlocking `vestibule_door`.
    10. Walk through `vestibule_door` into `ch01.signal_control.exit_trigger_vestibule`.
- **Alternative valid solutions:**
  - **Reverse Breaker Order (Echo on Breaker B, Player on Breaker A):** Recording the Echo pulling `breaker_b` and having Elias pull `breaker_a` during playback is completely symmetric and valid.
  - **Delayed Echo Recording (Playback Before Walking):** Starting recording at `breaker_a`, waiting ~240 ticks (4.0 s) before pulling `breaker_a`, stopping recording, immediately pressing Play at `breaker_a`, and walking to `breaker_b` during the 240-tick delay to pull `breaker_b` in sync with `&"echo.0"` (total recording ~`300` ticks, still well within the `≤ 900` tick target).
  - **Player Pulls Breaker B Slightly Before Echo Pulls Breaker A:** If Elias pulls `breaker_b` up to `179` ticks *before* `&"echo.0"` pulls `breaker_a`, `breaker_b` is still in `lever_state == &"engaged"` (`engage_ticks >= 1`) when `&"echo.0"` pulls `breaker_a`, which also satisfies simultaneous engagement.
- **Failure states:**
  - **Pulling Breakers Before Step 1:** Returns `&"unpowered"` (*"Main power bus is dead—stabilize the central surge capacitor first."*).
  - **Rewinding Frozen Capacitor:** Attempting `&"rewind"` on `surge_capacitor` while `is_frozen == true` returns `&"frozen"`.
  - **Echo Replay While Bus Unpowered:** If the player records a valid `breaker_a` pull while `bus_powered == true`, then unfreezes `surge_capacitor` so `bus_powered` drops to `false`, and replays the Echo, the replayed interaction on `breaker_a` fails `can_interact` with `&"unpowered"`, emits `echo_event_failed(tick, &"ch01.signal_control.breaker_a", &"unpowered")` (`ADR-008`), and leaves `breaker_a` open.
- **Reset behavior:**
  - **While `UNSOLVED`:** Interacting with `ch01.signal_control.reset_anchor_01` clears `&"echo.0"`, unfreezes `surge_capacitor` (`TemporalComponent.is_frozen = false`, `charge_state = &"discharged"`, `bus_powered = false`, `initial_history` preserved), and resets `breaker_a` and `breaker_b` to `"open"`.
  - **After `SOLVED` (`ADR-016`):** Once `CH1-PZ-004` is marked `SOLVED` in `PuzzleService`, `substation_grid.grid_online` and `vestibule_door` (`passage_blocked = false`) stay permanently open; neither `reset_anchor_01`, `echo_reset`, nor unfreezing `surge_capacitor` ever re-blocks the solved passage.
- **Hint 1 (nudge):** *"The two mezzanine breakers have no power while the central surge capacitor is dark. Check the wiring chart before touching a breaker."*
- **Hint 2 (rule reminder):** *"Step 1: Rewind the central surge capacitor, then Freeze it so the bus stays on. Step 2: use an Echo so breakers A and B are engaged at once. Leave breaker C alone."*
- **Hint 3 (next action):** *"Rewind and Freeze the central surge capacitor. Then record an Echo pulling the west mezzanine breaker, walk to the east mezzanine breaker, replay the Echo, and pull the east breaker at the same time."*
- **Success feedback:**
  - **Visual:** Both west and east power conduits surge into bright cyan-white; the Signal Control monitor bank boots with the station boundary map; `vestibule_door` retracts its dual bolts (**open chevron `[>>]`**).
  - **Audio:** Deep transformer hum locking in harmony, dual breaker clunk, and the wired intercom chime for The Operator's transmission.
- **Narrative reward:** Boots the Signal Control boundary display, triggers The Operator's urgent intercom call warning Elias away from the Atrium dispatch terminal, and opens the Upper Vestibule (`ch01.upper_vestibule`).
- **Accessibility considerations:**
  - **Two-step checkpointing:** Once Step 1 (`bus_powered == true` and `TemporalComponent.is_frozen == true`) is achieved, the player has unlimited time to set up Step 2 without any decay pressure.
  - **Color-independent cues:** Unpowered conduits display `[0] OFF` dashes; powered conduits display `[I] BUS LIVE` chevrons; engaged breakers show a **3-segment countdown bar `[III] → [II] → [I]`** above the lever plus a rhythmic 2 Hz metronome click so timing is clear both visually and audibly.
- **Acceptance tests:** See §6 below (`TEST-PZ004-01` through `TEST-PZ004-06`).

## 4. Echo Logic Track Specification (`ADR-008`)

- **Echo Limits (`ADR-008`):** Max active echoes = `1` (`&"echo.0"`). Max recording length = `1200` ticks (`20.0 s`).
- **Intended Solution Tick Budget:** **`45` to `90` ticks (`0.75–1.5 s`)** (or up to `300` ticks in the delayed-recording alternative) — well inside the `≤ 900` tick design target and `1200` tick cap.

## 5. Soft-Lock & Reachable Bad-State Analysis

| Reachable State | Potential Issue | Recovery Path (Zero Save Reload Required) |
|---|---|---|
| Player freezes `surge_capacitor` while it is `"discharged"` (`bus_powered == false`) | Can the player get stuck unable to rewind the capacitor? | **No.** Attempting `&"rewind"` returns `&"frozen"` with clear feedback; activating `&"freeze"` on `surge_capacitor` toggles `is_frozen = false` (or using `reset_anchor_01` resets it). |
| Player lets `surge_capacitor` decay for `240` ticks back to `"discharged"` without freezing it | Does `surge_capacitor` lose its `"charged"` history entry? | **No.** Transitioning from `"charged"` → `"discharged"` at `decay_ticks == 0` appends `"discharged"` to the ring buffer after `"charged"`, so activating `&"rewind"` again immediately restores `"charged"` (`decay_ticks = 240`). |
| Player records `&"echo.0"` pulling `breaker_a` while `bus_powered == true`, then unfreezes `surge_capacitor` so `bus_powered == false`, then plays the Echo | Could replaying a breaker interaction while unpowered corrupt state? | **No.** Per `ADR-008`, `InteractionService` validates `can_interact` at playback time, returns `&"unpowered"`, skips the event, and emits `echo_event_failed(tick, &"ch01.signal_control.breaker_a", &"unpowered")`. Re-stabilizing the capacitor allows the same Echo to be replayed successfully. |
| Player unfreezes `surge_capacitor` *after* synchronizing both breakers (`grid_online == true`) | Could unfreezing the capacitor after solving re-lock `vestibule_door` while the player is in the doorway? | **No.** Once `substation_grid.grid_online` becomes `true`, the main substation latch is permanent (`PuzzleService` state / room progression); `vestibule_door` stays open permanently. |

## 6. Acceptance Tests (`GIVEN / WHEN / THEN`)

### `TEST-PZ004-01` — Breakers reject interaction with `&"unpowered"` before capacitor is stabilized
- **GIVEN** `CH1-PZ-004` at `tick = 0` with `surge_capacitor.bus_powered == false`
- **WHEN** `InteractionService.request(&"player", &"ch01.signal_control.breaker_a", &"use")` is called
- **THEN** the result has `ok == false` and `reason == &"unpowered"`, and `breaker_a.lever_state` remains `&"open"`.

### `TEST-PZ004-02` — Rewind followed by Freeze on `surge_capacitor` locks `bus_powered == true`
- **GIVEN** `CH1-PZ-004` at `tick = 0`
- **WHEN** `InteractionService.request(&"player", &"ch01.signal_control.surge_capacitor", &"rewind")` is called at `tick = 10` (`decay_ticks = 240`), `SimClock.step(40)` advances to `tick = 50` (`decay_ticks = 200`), and `InteractionService.request(&"player", &"ch01.signal_control.surge_capacitor", &"freeze")` is called, followed by `SimClock.step(500)`
- **THEN** at `tick = 550`, `surge_capacitor.is_frozen == true`, `surge_capacitor.bus_powered == true`, `surge_capacitor.decay_ticks == 200`, and `substation_grid.step_1_stabilized == true`.

### `TEST-PZ004-03` — Single breaker engagement expires after 180 ticks if the second breaker is not pulled
- **GIVEN** `surge_capacitor.bus_powered == true` and `is_frozen == true`
- **WHEN** `InteractionService.request(&"player", &"ch01.signal_control.breaker_a", &"use")` is called at `tick = 100` and `SimClock.step(180)` advances to `tick = 280` without `breaker_b` being pulled
- **THEN** at `tick = 279` `breaker_a.lever_state == &"engaged"`, and at `tick = 280` `breaker_a.lever_state == &"open"` and `substation_grid.grid_online == false`.

### `TEST-PZ004-04` — Echo pulling Breaker A + Player pulling Breaker B within 180 ticks locks `grid_online == true`
- **GIVEN** `surge_capacitor.bus_powered == true` and `is_frozen == true`, and an Echo recorded `[rel_tick: 30, kind: &"interact", target_id: &"ch01.signal_control.breaker_a", interaction_id: &"use", payload: {}]` (stopped at `rel_tick = 60`), after which `breaker_a` timed out back to `&"open"`
- **WHEN** `EchoService.start_playback()` is called at `tick = 300`, `SimClock.step(30)` advances to `tick = 330` (`&"echo.0"` engages `breaker_a`), `SimClock.step(50)` advances to `tick = 380`, and `InteractionService.request(&"player", &"ch01.signal_control.breaker_b", &"use")` is called at `tick = 380`
- **THEN** both `breaker_a` and `breaker_b` are `&"engaged"`, `substation_grid.grid_online` becomes `true`, and `vestibule_door.passage_blocked` becomes `false`.

### `TEST-PZ004-05` — Echo replay while capacitor is unpowered emits `echo_event_failed` with `&"unpowered"`
- **GIVEN** an Echo has recorded `[rel_tick: 20, kind: &"interact", target_id: &"ch01.signal_control.breaker_a", interaction_id: &"use", payload: {}]`, and `surge_capacitor` is subsequently unfrozen and allowed to decay to `bus_powered == false`
- **WHEN** `EchoService.start_playback()` is called and `SimClock.step(20)` executes the recorded event
- **THEN** `EchoService` emits `echo_event_failed` with `target_id == &"ch01.signal_control.breaker_a"` and `reason == &"unpowered"`, and `breaker_a.lever_state` remains `&"open"`.

### `TEST-PZ004-06` — Entering vestibule exit trigger after `grid_online == true` solves `CH1-PZ-004`
- **GIVEN** `substation_grid.grid_online == true` and `vestibule_door.passage_blocked == false`
- **WHEN** `InteractionService.trigger_enter(&"player", &"ch01.signal_control.exit_trigger_vestibule")` is called
- **THEN** `PuzzleService.is_solved(&"CH1-PZ-004")` becomes `true`.
