# Puzzle Specification: CH1-PZ-005 — The Displaced Corridor

## 1. Standard Puzzle Specification (`PUZZLE_DESIGN.md` §2 & §4)

- **Puzzle ID:** `CH1-PZ-005`
- **Name:** The Displaced Corridor
- **Chapter / Room:** Chapter 1 (*The Forgotten Station*) / Upper Vestibule (`ch01.upper_vestibule`, Beat 08)
- **Narrative purpose:** Deliver Chapter 1's single **spatial foreshadow puzzle** (`SD-1`, `ADR-009`) using **doorway reassignment only** (no room rotation, gravity, or folding). Show Elias that as he nears the surface exit, the ELYSIUM containment field has frayed spatial thresholds into logical index connections—foreshadowing Chapter 3 (*The Infinite Library*).
- **Player objective:** Use `Trace` on the vestibule route board and archway transoms to read the spatial index glyphs, then operate the two route-junction levers (`ch01.upper_vestibule.junction_lever_a` and `ch01.upper_vestibule.junction_lever_b`) in the valid sequence to reassign `ch01.upper_vestibule:door_north` to `ch01.station_atrium:door_entry_south` and enter the Station Atrium.
- **Required abilities:** `Trace` (plus standard physical `&"use"` interaction on the two route-junction levers)
- **Required objects:**
  - `ch01.upper_vestibule.route_board` (Central illuminated spatial index map & `Trace` locus)
  - `ch01.upper_vestibule.junction_lever_a` (West Junction Relay Lever — controls Loop / Bus-02 uncoupling)
  - `ch01.upper_vestibule.junction_lever_b` (East Junction Relay Lever — controls Niche / Atrium feed on Bus-02)
  - `ch01.upper_vestibule.door_south_presenter` (South Entry Archway connected to `ch01.signal_control:door_exit_north`)
  - `ch01.upper_vestibule.door_west_presenter` (West Archway presenter)
  - `ch01.upper_vestibule.door_east_presenter` (East Archway presenter)
  - `ch01.upper_vestibule.door_north_presenter` (North Archway presenter toward Station Atrium)
  - `ch01.upper_vestibule.safe_anchor_center` (`Marker2D` safe relocation anchor at the center of the vestibule when `SpatialGraph.changed` fires)
  - `ch01.upper_vestibule.reset_anchor_01` (Spatial index master reset pedestal)
  - `ch01.station_atrium.entry_trigger_atrium` (`Area2D` completion trigger inside `ch01.station_atrium` beyond `door_entry_south`)
- **Initial room state:**
  - Elias enters `ch01.upper_vestibule` through `door_south` from `ch01.signal_control`.
  - Three ornate transit archways stand ahead: **West (`door_west`)**, **North (`door_north`)**, and **East (`door_east`)**.
  - Walking through **North (`door_north`)** immediately walks Elias back out of **West (`door_west`)** into the same room (`State 0` loop).
  - Walking through **East (`door_east`)** leads into a small side Inspection Niche (`ch01.vestibule_niche:door_in`), where a barred archway (`ch01.vestibule_niche:door_out`) lets Elias glimpse the grand Station Atrium through a collapsed iron grille he cannot physically walk through, alongside an ELYSIUM Spatial Index plaque.
  - `junction_lever_a` is in `relay_state = &"loop"`, and `junction_lever_b` is in `relay_state = &"niche"`.
- **Rules the player must discover:**
  1. Doorways in the Upper Vestibule do not connect by fixed Euclidean geometry; they connect according to the active doorway graph displayed on `ch01.upper_vestibule.route_board` and above each archway transom.
  2. Using `Trace` on `route_board` reveals the underlying index wiring:
     - **Lever A (`junction_lever_a`)** switches `door_north` off the **West Loop (`[◁] Index 01`)** and onto the **North-East Relay Bus (`[◇] Index 02`)**.
     - **Lever B (`junction_lever_b`)** switches the **Station Atrium (`[▲] Atrium`)** from the blocked niche grille (`niche:door_out`) onto the active **North-East Relay Bus (`[◇] Index 02`)**.
  3. **Graph Validity Rule (`ADR-009`):** A doorway cannot be assigned two destinations at once. If the player pulls **Lever B first** while **Lever A** is still locking `door_north` to `door_west`, `SpatialGraph.apply()` rejects the invalid transform with `&"invalid_graph"` (duplicate edge conflict on `door_north`) and flashes the conflict line on `route_board`. The player must **pull Lever A first (uncouple the West Loop), then pull Lever B (route North Archway to Station Atrium)**.
- **Step-by-step intended solution:**
  1. Walk through `door_north` once (or inspect `route_board`) to observe that `door_north` loops out of `door_west`.
  2. Activate `Trace` (`&"trace"`) on `ch01.upper_vestibule.route_board` (and/or inspect the plaque inside `ch01.vestibule_niche` via `door_east`) to reveal the two-stage relay diagram (`[◁] Loop` → `[◇] Bus 02` → `[▲] Atrium`).
  3. Interact (`&"use"`) with `ch01.upper_vestibule.junction_lever_a`: `SpatialGraph.apply(T_A_UNCOUPLE)` transitions the graph from **State 0** to **State 1** (`door_north` now connects to `vestibule_niche:door_in`, and `door_west` connects to `door_east`). The transom glyph above `door_north` shifts from `[◁]` to `[◇]`.
  4. Interact (`&"use"`) with `ch01.upper_vestibule.junction_lever_b`: `SpatialGraph.apply(T_B_ATRIUM)` transitions the graph from **State 1** to **State 2** (`door_north` now connects directly to `ch01.station_atrium:door_entry_south`). The transom glyph above `door_north` shifts from `[◇]` to `[▲]`.
  5. Walk north through `ch01.upper_vestibule:door_north` to emerge from `ch01.station_atrium:door_entry_south` and trigger `ch01.station_atrium.entry_trigger_atrium`.
- **Alternative valid solutions:**
  - **Direct Lever Sequence Without Entering Niche:** A player who uses `Trace` on `route_board` in the main vestibule can immediately deduce the `Lever A` → `Lever B` sequence without walking into `ch01.vestibule_niche`.
  - **Experimental Discovery after Rejected Lever B:** A player who pulls `Lever B` first receives explicit visual/audio rejection feedback pointing to `Lever A`'s loop lock on `door_north`, then pulls `Lever A` followed by `Lever B`.
- **Failure states:**
  - **Pulling Lever B while Lever A is in `&"loop"` (`State 0`):** `SpatialGraph.apply(T_B_CONFLICT)` fails validation (`&"invalid_graph"` — duplicate edge on `door_north` and dangling edge on `niche:door_out`). State remains **State 0** with zero corruption.
  - **Pulling Lever A while Lever B is in `&"atrium"` (`State 2`):** `SpatialGraph.apply(T_A_CONFLICT)` fails validation (`&"invalid_graph"` — would leave `station_atrium:door_entry_south` dangling). State remains **State 2** (the player can either walk straight through the open `door_north` into the Atrium or press `reset_anchor_01` to reset both levers atomically to State 0).
  - **Using Rewind / Freeze on Levers or Archways:** Returns `&"unsupported"` (*"Spatial index relays are anchored outside local time—switch them manually."*).
- **Reset behavior:**
  - **While `UNSOLVED`:** Interacting (`&"reset"` / `&"use"`) with `ch01.upper_vestibule.reset_anchor_01` applies the atomic `T_RESET` graph transform, restoring **State 0** (`junction_lever_a = &"loop"`, `junction_lever_b = &"niche"`). If `player` (or `&"echo.0"`) is in an affected doorway threshold or inside `ch01.vestibule_niche` during any `SpatialGraph.changed` signal, `SpatialGraph` presenter relocation moves the player to `ch01.upper_vestibule.safe_anchor_center` (`ADR-009`, `ADR-016`).
  - **After `SOLVED` (`ADR-016`):** Once `CH1-PZ-005` reaches `SOLVED` in `PuzzleService`, the graph locks permanently in **State 2** (`door_north ↔ ch01.station_atrium:door_entry_south`); neither the levers nor `reset_anchor_01` ever re-blocks the solved passage.
- **Hint 1 (nudge):** *"The relays are dead. Something has to hold the standby plate while you reach the levers."*
- **Hint 2 (rule reminder):** *"An Echo can stand on the plate. The North Archway cannot join the Atrium while it is still looped to the West Archway."*
- **Hint 3 (next action):** *"Record an Echo on the standby plate and replay it, then pull the West lever first and the East lever second, and walk north."*
- **Success feedback:**
  - **Visual:** The spatial ripple inside the North Archway clears to reveal a direct view of the Station Atrium steps; the transom glyph above `door_north` locks onto the **Atrium Crown Triangle `[▲]`**; the route board shows a solid unbroken line from `door_north` to `ch01.station_atrium`.
  - **Audio:** Deep sub-bass spatial realignment thrum followed by a resonant brass station bell from the Atrium beyond.
- **Narrative reward:** Grants entry to the Station Atrium (`ch01.station_atrium`) for Chapter 1's major revelation (Beat 09, *Ledger 23:59*) and unlocks the optional lore note in `ch01.vestibule_niche` explaining ELYSIUM threshold indexing.
- **Accessibility considerations:**
  - **Distinct shape glyphs (color-independent):** Every doorway and route-board line uses large, high-contrast geometric symbols in addition to color:
    - West Loop: **Left Triangle `[◁]`**
    - Relay Bus 02 (Niche): **Diamond `[◇]`**
    - Station Atrium: **Upward Crown Triangle `[▲]`**
    - South Entry (Signal Control): **Square Anchor `[■]`**
  - **Reduced motion support:** When Reduced Effects is enabled in `Settings`, doorway reassignment swaps the portal preview with a clean cross-dissolve rather than a distortion warp shader.
- **Acceptance tests:** See §6 below (`TEST-PZ005-01` through `TEST-PZ005-06`).

## 5. Soft-Lock & Reachable Bad-State Analysis

| Reachable State | Potential Issue | Recovery Path (Zero Save Reload Required) |
|---|---|---|
| Player walks into `ch01.vestibule_niche` via `door_east` (State 0) or `door_north` (State 1) | Can the player get trapped inside `ch01.vestibule_niche`? | **No.** The levers `junction_lever_a` and `junction_lever_b` are located in `ch01.upper_vestibule`, so the graph cannot change while the player is alone in the niche (and even if an Echo or reset triggers `SpatialGraph.changed` while the player is in `ch01.vestibule_niche`, `ADR-009` presenter rebuild moves the player to `ch01.upper_vestibule.safe_anchor_center`). Walking back out of `niche:door_in` always returns Elias to `ch01.upper_vestibule`. |
| Player records an Echo toggling a lever while Elias stands inside `ch01.vestibule_niche` | Could an Echo isolating `vestibule_niche` in State 2 trap Elias inside the niche? | **No.** Per `ADR-009` (*"A player in an affected room is moved to a safe anchor"*), when `SpatialGraph.apply()` commits State 2 while `player` is in `ch01.vestibule_niche` (whose doorway edge changed), `player` is automatically relocated to `ch01.upper_vestibule.safe_anchor_center`. |
| Player wants to return to State 0 from State 1 or State 2 | How does the player reset the graph if Lever A is locked in State 2? | **Two paths:** (1) Pull `junction_lever_b` (`&"atrium"` → `&"niche"`, returning to State 1) and then pull `junction_lever_a` (`&"uncoupled"` → `&"loop"`, returning to State 0); or (2) interact with `ch01.upper_vestibule.reset_anchor_01`, which atomically applies `T_RESET` to return directly to State 0. |

## 6. Acceptance Tests (`GIVEN / WHEN / THEN`)

### `TEST-PZ005-01` — Initial State 0 loops `door_north` to `door_west`
- **GIVEN** `CH1-PZ-005` initialized at `tick = 0` in **State 0** (`junction_lever_a.relay_state == &"loop"`, `junction_lever_b.relay_state == &"niche"`)
- **WHEN** `SpatialGraph.get_destination(&"ch01.upper_vestibule", &"door_north")` is queried
- **THEN** it returns `{room_id: &"ch01.upper_vestibule", door_id: &"door_west"}`.

### `TEST-PZ005-02` — Pulling `junction_lever_b` in State 0 is rejected with `&"invalid_graph"` and leaves graph unchanged
- **GIVEN** `CH1-PZ-005` in **State 0** (`junction_lever_a.relay_state == &"loop"`)
- **WHEN** `InteractionService.request(&"player", &"ch01.upper_vestibule.junction_lever_b", &"use")` is called
- **THEN** the result has `ok == false` and `reason == &"invalid_graph"`, `SpatialGraph.changed` is **not** emitted, `junction_lever_b.relay_state` remains `&"niche"`, and `door_north` still maps to `ch01.upper_vestibule:door_west`.

### `TEST-PZ005-03` — Pulling `junction_lever_a` transitions to State 1, and pulling `junction_lever_b` transitions to State 2
- **GIVEN** `CH1-PZ-005` in **State 0**
- **WHEN** `InteractionService.request(&"player", &"ch01.upper_vestibule.junction_lever_a", &"use")` is called, followed by `InteractionService.request(&"player", &"ch01.upper_vestibule.junction_lever_b", &"use")`
- **THEN** both requests return `ok == true`, `SpatialGraph.changed` is emitted twice, `graph_state_id == 2`, and `SpatialGraph.get_destination(&"ch01.upper_vestibule", &"door_north")` returns `{room_id: &"ch01.station_atrium", door_id: &"door_entry_south"}`.

### `TEST-PZ005-04` — Direct `SpatialGraph.apply()` with severing entry edge is rejected with `&"critical_path_unreachable"`
- **GIVEN** `SpatialGraph` in **State 0**
- **WHEN** `SpatialGraph.apply(T_SEVER_ENTRY)` is called (attempting to loop `ch01.signal_control:door_exit_north` to itself and `ch01.upper_vestibule:door_south` to `door_west`)
- **THEN** `apply()` returns `{ok: false, reason: &"critical_path_unreachable"}` and all existing edges remain unchanged.

### `TEST-PZ005-05` — Player inside affected room `ch01.vestibule_niche` is moved to `safe_anchor_center` on graph change (`ADR-009`)
- **GIVEN** `SpatialGraph` is in **State 1** and `player` is currently located inside `ch01.vestibule_niche`
- **WHEN** `SpatialGraph.apply(T_B_ATRIUM)` (or `reset_anchor_01`) commits a change to `ch01.vestibule_niche`'s doorway edges
- **THEN** `player` is relocated to `ch01.upper_vestibule.safe_anchor_center` and is not stranded inside `ch01.vestibule_niche`.

### `TEST-PZ005-06` — Traversing `door_north` in State 2 enters `ch01.station_atrium` and marks `CH1-PZ-005` solved
- **GIVEN** `CH1-PZ-005` is in **State 2** (`door_north` connected to `ch01.station_atrium:door_entry_south`)
- **WHEN** `&"player"` traverses `door_north` and triggers `InteractionService.trigger_enter(&"player", &"ch01.station_atrium.entry_trigger_atrium")`
- **THEN** `PuzzleService.is_solved(&"CH1-PZ-005")` becomes `true`.
