# Puzzle Specification: CH1-PZ-006 — Last Ticket (Trace + Rewind)

**Task:** TLF-047 phase 2 (Step 1) · **Concept:** `design/ch01/CH01_EXPANSION.md` §2 MP-A (approved `e038e45`) · **Author:** design agent (Claude Code, Sonnet 5.5), standing in for Argon

## 1. Standard Puzzle Specification (`PUZZLE_DESIGN.md` §2)

- **Puzzle ID:** `CH1-PZ-006`
- **Name:** Last Ticket
- **Chapter / Room:** Chapter 1 (*The Forgotten Station*) / West Concourse, east half (`ch01.concourse_west`, Beat 03, sub-beats 03a-03b)
- **Narrative purpose:** The first puzzle where `Trace` and `Rewind` are both *required*: Trace is the only source of the values, Rewind the only way to set them. It shows the "five minutes" after the order: people kept queueing and the ticket machines kept issuing.
- **Player objective:** Open the staff door at the east end of the concourse by showing it the one ticket it honours: set the three ticket-machine dials (LINE, PLATFORM, TIME) to that ticket's values and press the validator.
- **Required abilities:** `Trace` (information) **and** `Rewind` (acting). Combo of the chapter's first two abilities.
- **Required objects:** `n06_red_coat`, `n07_briefcase`, `n08_father`, `n09_teen` (`TraceMemory`), `doc_ticket_terms`, `doc_evac_notice` (existing, the 23:54 order), `call_box_09` (existing, new nudge `OP_B03_06`), `dial_line`, `dial_platform`, `dial_time` (`HistoryDial`), `ticket_validator`, `staff_door`, `reset_anchor_02`.
- **Initial room state:** `CH1-PZ-001` is `SOLVED` (Rewind unlocked, turnstile open). The dials sit at their newest entry (index 5): LINE `HILL ROAD`, PLATFORM `PLAT 5`, TIME `23:57`. `staff_door` is closed (the east exit). The four passengers are untraced.
- **Rules the player must discover:**
  1. The staff door honours exactly one ticket: **Orchard Gate**, bought **after the 23:54 order** (terms sign + bulletin).
  2. Each queue member holds a different ticket; `Trace` shows its line, platform and time.
  3. The dials cannot be turned by hand (`use`: "The dial only turns back with the watch."). `Rewind` steps a dial one entry back; the history is **one-way** and **not aligned** between dials.
  4. A wrong combination gets "No such ticket." with no hint which dial is wrong. The reset console puts all dials back.
- **Step-by-step intended solution:**
  1. Read the bulletin (Beat 03, existing) and the ticket terms (`doc_ticket_terms`): Orchard Gate only, bought after 23:54.
  2. `Trace` the four passengers: red coat (Harbour, P1, 23:41), briefcase (Central, P2, 23:49), father (Orchard Gate, P3, **23:53**), teenager (**Orchard Gate, P1, 23:55**). Each adds a journal observation.
  3. Conclude: the teenager's ticket is the only one on the right line **and** after 23:54 (the father's is one minute too early).
  4. At the dial wall with `Rewind` selected: LINE rewound 3 steps (to `ORCHARD GATE`), PLATFORM rewound 2 steps (to `PLAT 1`), TIME rewound 4 steps (to `23:55`). Each dial shows its entry as it moves.
  5. `use` the validator: "Ticket accepted." The staff door unlocks (latched), `CH1-PZ-006` is `SOLVED`.
- **Dial data (index 0 = oldest, index 5 = tick 0):**

  | Index | LINE (`PZ006_LINE_i`) | PLATFORM (`PZ006_PLAT_i`) | TIME (`PZ006_TIME_i`) |
  |--:|---|---|---|
  | 0 | DOCKSIDE | PLAT 6 | 23:38 |
  | 1 | HARBOUR | PLAT 4 | **23:55** (target) |
  | 2 | **ORCHARD GATE** (target) | PLAT 2 | 23:49 |
  | 3 | CENTRAL | **PLAT 1** (target) | 23:41 |
  | 4 | HOLLOW QUAY | PLAT 3 | 23:53 |
  | 5 (start) | HILL ROAD | PLAT 5 | 23:57 |

  Target indexes `[2, 3, 1]`. Rewinds needed: `[3, 2, 4]` (9 clicks). The passengers' tickets as dial indexes `[LINE, PLATFORM, TIME]`: red coat `[1,3,3]`, briefcase `[3,2,2]`, father `[2,4,4]`, teenager `[2,3,1]` (target). None is a constant triple (the histories are deliberately not aligned).
- **Alternative valid solutions:** none that avoid the information; every other combination is rejected.
- **Failure states:**
  * Mixed or wrong values (including the father's ticket): `reason == &"invalid_ticket"` (same for all).
  * Rewinding past a target: the dial cannot go forward; the player uses `reset_anchor_02` (`ResetAnchor`, all three dials back to index 5) or re-sets from there.
  * `Rewind` at index 0: `&"no_history"`.
  * `use` on a dial: `&"jammed"`; on the staff door while closed: `&"locked"`.
- **Reset behavior:** while `UNSOLVED`, `reset_anchor_02` calls `PuzzleService.reset_puzzle(&"CH1-PZ-006")` → the three dials `reset_to_initial()` (index 5). After `SOLVED` the door stays open, the dials stay where they are and `reset_anchor_02` returns `&"already_open"`.
- **Hint 1 (nudge):** *"The staff door wants one particular ticket. Who in the queue bought theirs after the order, and for which line?"*
- **Hint 2 (rule reminder):** *"Trace each person in the queue to see line, platform and time. The notice sets the line and the time. The dials only turn back, one step per Rewind."*
- **Hint 3 (next action):** *"Rewind the dials to Orchard Gate, platform 1 and 23:55 (the teenager's ticket), then use the validator."*
- **Success feedback:** Visual: three green chevrons on the validator, the door lifts. Audio: a stamp, then a servo. Journal: `JRN_PZ006_SOLVED` (theory). Elias: no line.
- **Narrative reward:** the way to the Maintenance Bay; six journal observations (four tickets, terms, outcome). The Operator's nudge (`OP_B03_06`) is part of the call box script before `OP_B03_05`.
- **Accessibility considerations:** each dial value has a text label and a glyph (line: shape; platform: pips; time: digits); no colour is needed; no timers; the wrong-ticket message is text.
- **Estimate (model §1.3):** 5 d (125 s: terms + four passengers) + 7 e (84 s) + 2 w (30 s) + 1 r (20 s) = 259 s = **4.3 min design**, 2.6 min floor. ✔ ≥ 3.
- **Dependent steps (≥ 3):** read terms → Trace passengers → decide the ticket → rewind three dials → validate → walk through.
- **Hub:** the terms sign, `call_box_09` and the four passengers can be done in any order; nothing needs backtracking (all in the east half or already passed).

## 6. Shortcut analysis (RISK-018)

| Shortcut | Excluded by | Negative test (real input) |
|---|---|---|
| Set the dials by hand | `use` on a dial is `&"jammed"`; no other state source exists | `TEST-PZ006-03` |
| Skip Trace (find the values elsewhere) | the ticket values exist only in `MEM_N06…N09`; `doc_ticket_terms` and `doc_evac_notice` name only the line and the time **rule**, never a platform or a time value | `TEST-PZ006-08`: text grep |
| Skip Rewind (reach the door another way) | the staff door is the only exit east (E2) and its Blocker fills the aperture; the player has only Trace and Rewind at this point (availability) | `TEST-PZ006-09`: walk at the aperture for 300 ticks with a wrong ticket → never enters `door_exit_east` |
| Brute force | 6 × 6 × 6 = 216 combinations; the dials are one-way and not index-aligned; each full sweep of one dial needs a reset (20 s): expected ≈ 22 min vs. 4.3 min by reading; no partial feedback | `TEST-PZ006-05`: all non-target triples give the same reason |
| "All dials at the same index" trick | the target `[2, 3, 1]` is not a constant triple; no passenger's ticket is a constant triple | `TEST-PZ006-06` |
| Use the father's ticket (Orchard Gate, P3, 23:53) | indexes `[2, 4, 4]` ≠ `[2, 3, 1]` | `TEST-PZ006-07` |

## 7. Acceptance tests (`GIVEN / WHEN / THEN`)

* **TEST-PZ006-01 — correct ticket solves.** GIVEN dials at `[2,3,1]`; WHEN `request(player, ticket_validator, use)`; THEN `ok == true`, `staff_door.passage_blocked == false`, `PuzzleService.is_solved(&"CH1-PZ-006")`, `JRN_PZ006_SOLVED` journaled.
* **TEST-PZ006-02 — rewinding steps the dial back.** GIVEN `dial_line` at index 5; WHEN `rewind` three times; THEN `capture_state() == {"index": 2}` and the label is `ORCHARD GATE`; a fourth `rewind` gives index 1; at index 0 `rewind` returns `&"no_history"`.
* **TEST-PZ006-03 — `use` on a dial is jammed.** `reason == &"jammed"`, index unchanged.
* **TEST-PZ006-04 — initial state is wrong.** GIVEN dials at `[5,5,5]`; WHEN the validator is used; THEN `reason == &"invalid_ticket"`, door closed.
* **TEST-PZ006-05 — no partial feedback.** GIVEN each of the 215 non-target triples reachable by Rewind (or a sample of 40 chosen so every dial is wrong alone, in pairs and in all three); THEN the result `reason` and feedback key are identical.
* **TEST-PZ006-06 — no constant triple matches.** THEN `target != [k,k,k]` for every `k`, and no passenger ticket maps to a constant triple.
* **TEST-PZ006-07 — the father's ticket is rejected.** GIVEN dials at `[2,4,4]` (Orchard Gate, PLAT 3, 23:53); THEN `&"invalid_ticket"`.
* **TEST-PZ006-08 — values live only in Trace memories.** GIVEN `ch01_text.csv`; THEN `PLAT 1`/`23:55` as a ticket (i.e. a platform or time paired with a passenger) appear only in `MEM_N0[6-9]_*`, `JRN_MEM_N0[6-9]` and the `PZ006_*` dial labels; `DOC_TICKET_TERMS_BODY_1` and `DOC_EVAC_NOTICE_BODY_1` contain neither.
* **TEST-PZ006-09 — staff door is the only way (real colliders).** GIVEN the door closed; WHEN the player walks at the east aperture for 300 ticks; THEN `door_exit_east` is never entered.
* **TEST-PZ006-10 — reset.** GIVEN dials rewound; WHEN `reset_anchor_02` is used; THEN all indexes are 5 and the history has 5 entries again; `echo` state untouched.
* **TEST-PZ006-11 — latch.** GIVEN `SOLVED`; WHEN saved and loaded; THEN the door is open and `reset_anchor_02` returns `&"already_open"`.
* **TEST-PZ006-12 — save keeps dial history.** GIVEN `dial_time` at index 2; WHEN saved and loaded; THEN `get_history_cursor()` reproduces index 2 and one more `rewind` goes to index 1.
* **TEST-PZ006-13 — hubs are order-free.** GIVEN any order of (terms, call box, four Traces); THEN the dials/validator behave identically (no hidden prerequisite flag).
* **TEST-PZ006-14 — Rewind needs the unlock.** GIVEN Rewind locked; THEN `choose_watch_target(&"rewind")` is unreachable because `ability_next` cannot select it (covers the unlock gate).
