# Puzzle Specification: CH1-PZ-001 — The Fractured Turnstile

## 1. Standard Puzzle Specification (`PUZZLE_DESIGN.md` §2)

- **Puzzle ID:** `CH1-PZ-001`
- **Name:** The Fractured Turnstile
- **Chapter / Room:** Chapter 1 (*The Forgotten Station*) / West Turnstile Gate (`ch01.concourse_west`, entry threshold from `ch01.arrival_platform`, Beat 02)
- **Narrative purpose:** Introduce the damaged pocket watch (`global.pocket_watch`) as a forensic and localized temporal tool rather than a world-reset button. Show that someone fled through the West Turnstile moments before a ceiling conduit collapsed at `23:59`.
- **Player objective:** Inspect the temporal residue at the crushed turnstile barrier using `Trace`, then apply `Rewind` to restore the barrier arm to its pre-arrival open state and enter the West Concourse.
- **Required abilities:** `Trace`, `Rewind`
- **Required objects:**
  - `ch01.concourse_west.turnstile_gate` (Temporal turnstile barrier)
  - `ch01.concourse_west.turnstile_trace_anchor` (Residual trace locus at the turnstile)
  - `ch01.concourse_west.support_pillar_01` (Non-temporal structural column next to the gate, used to teach `&"unsupported"` feedback)
  - `ch01.concourse_west.reset_anchor_01` (Manual gate diagnostic box / puzzle reset anchor)
  - `ch01.concourse_west.exit_trigger_concourse` (`Area2D` completion trigger beyond the turnstile)
- **Initial room state:**
  - Elias enters the threshold from `ch01.arrival_platform` holding `global.pocket_watch`.
  - `ch01.concourse_west.turnstile_gate` is in `arm_state = &"collapsed"`, blocking the 2-tile-wide isometric passage (`passage_blocked = true`). Amber sparks hang motionless around its bent rotor.
  - A shimmering cyan-gold distortion (`ch01.concourse_west.turnstile_trace_anchor`) hovers directly over the turnstile card reader.
- **Rules the player must discover:**
  1. Using `Trace` on a shimmering temporal distortion reveals residual silhouettes/events showing how an object reached its current state.
  2. Only objects with a temporal fracture aura (declaring `REWIND` capability) can be rewound; static architecture (`ch01.concourse_west.support_pillar_01`) rejects `Rewind` with clear feedback (`&"unsupported"`).
  3. Using `Rewind` steps a supported object backward to its prior recorded state (`initial_history` at `tick -1`), clearing physical collision once `arm_state == &"open"`.
- **Step-by-step intended solution:**
  1. Walk to the blocked turnstile (`ch01.concourse_west.turnstile_gate`) and press `interact` (`&"use"`): receive feedback that the rotor is physically jammed (`&"jammed"`).
  2. Open the pocket watch (`watch`), select `Trace`, and activate `&"trace"` on `ch01.concourse_west.turnstile_trace_anchor` (or directly on `turnstile_gate`, which forwards/reveals the trace): observe the residual silhouette of a figure Vaulting through the open gate just before the conduit fell, highlighting the turnstile's rewindable state ring.
  3. Cycle pocket watch ability (`ability_next` / `ability_prev`) to `Rewind` and activate `&"rewind"` on `ch01.concourse_west.turnstile_gate`.
  4. `turnstile_gate` restores its `tick -1` state (`arm_state = &"open"`, `passage_blocked = false`), retracting the barrier collision.
  5. Walk through the opened turnstile into `ch01.concourse_west.exit_trigger_concourse` to complete the puzzle.
- **Alternative valid solutions:**
  - **Direct Rewind (Skipping Trace):** A player who immediately selects `Rewind` and targets `ch01.concourse_west.turnstile_gate` without activating `Trace` first still succeeds (`Rewind` does not hard-gate on `trace_revealed == true`, preserving systemic consistency per Design Pillar 1). If they skip `Trace` here, the HUD keeps a subtle non-blocking prompt reminding them how `Trace` works on the turnstile anchor.
- **Failure states:**
  - Attempting physical `&"use"` on `turnstile_gate` while `arm_state == &"collapsed"` returns `&"jammed"`.
  - Attempting `&"rewind"` on `support_pillar_01` returns `&"unsupported"`.
  - Attempting `&"rewind"` on `turnstile_gate` after it is already in `arm_state == &"open"` returns `&"no_history"` (its state remains `&"open"`).
- **Reset behavior:**
  - **While `UNSOLVED`:** Interacting (`&"reset"` / `&"use"`) with `ch01.concourse_west.reset_anchor_01` restores `turnstile_gate` to its tick-0 state (`collapsed`, `passage_blocked = true`) and resets its history cursor to `tick 0` (`initial_history` at `tick -1` preserved). If the player overlaps the gate footprint during reset, the player is moved to `ch01.concourse_west.safe_anchor_east` (`ADR-016`).
  - **After `SOLVED` (`ADR-016`):** Once `CH1-PZ-001` reaches `SOLVED` in `PuzzleService`, the turnstile passage remains permanently open (`arm_state = &"open"`, `passage_blocked = false`); activating `reset_anchor_01` never re-blocks the solved passage.
- **Hint 1 (nudge):** *"The turnstile wasn't always crushed. Trace the gate, then look at who is standing beside it."*
- **Hint 2 (rule reminder):** *"Something fell on the gate after someone passed. It pins the turnstile, and it has a past too."*
- **Hint 3 (next action):** *"Rewind the fallen conduit back onto its bracket, then Rewind the turnstile, then walk through."*
- **Success feedback:**
  - **Visual:** Bent turnstile arm smoothly snaps backward in a brass-gold rewind trail to its retracted position; the gate status indicator changes from a **crossed diamond `[X]`** (amber) to an **open chevron `[>>]`** (cyan).
  - **Audio:** Reversed metallic crunch followed by a clean mechanical chime and a two-note pocket-watch resonance chord.
- **Narrative reward:** Grants access to the West Concourse (`ch01.concourse_west`) and logs Journal Observation `JRN_CH1_TURNSTILE_TRACE` (*"Someone else passed through the West Turnstile seconds before the station froze at 23:59."*).
- **Accessibility considerations:**
  - **Color-independent state:** Gate barrier displays a distinct geometric icon (**crossed diamond `[X]`** when collapsed/blocked vs. **double chevron `[>>]`** when open) in addition to amber/cyan indicator lights.
  - **Audio-visual parity:** The `Trace` silhouette and `Rewind` completion both pair distinct audio stingers with high-contrast visual glyphs and subtitle captions.
  - **No timing pressure:** `CH1-PZ-001` is completely untimed.
- **Acceptance tests:** See §5 below (`TEST-PZ001-01` through `TEST-PZ001-05`).

## 4. Soft-Lock & Reachable Bad-State Analysis

| Reachable State | Potential Issue | Recovery Path (Zero Save Reload Required) |
|---|---|---|
| Player activates `&"rewind"` repeatedly on `turnstile_gate` after it opens | Could a second `rewind` break or re-close the gate? | **No.** At `tick -1` (`arm_state == &"open"`), `cursor == 0`; further `&"rewind"` requests return `&"no_history"` without altering state (`ADR-007` Am. 2). |
| Player skips `Trace` and immediately uses `Rewind` on `turnstile_gate` | Could skipping `Trace` block puzzle completion or the journal clue? | **No.** `turnstile_gate` opens normally via `Rewind`, and `turnstile_trace_anchor` remains inspectable before or after opening the gate. |
| Player triggers `reset_anchor_01` before or after solving `CH1-PZ-001` | Could resetting trap the player inside the gate collider or re-block the solved passage? | **No.** While `UNSOLVED`, if `player` overlaps the gate footprint during reset, the player is moved to `ch01.concourse_west.safe_anchor_east` first. Once `SOLVED` (`ADR-016`), `reset_anchor_01` never re-blocks the solved turnstile passage. |

## 5. Acceptance Tests (`GIVEN / WHEN / THEN`)

### `TEST-PZ001-01` — Physical interaction on collapsed turnstile fails with `&"jammed"`
- **GIVEN** a fresh session of `CH1-PZ-001` at `tick = 0` where `ch01.concourse_west.turnstile_gate` has `arm_state == &"collapsed"` and `passage_blocked == true`
- **WHEN** `InteractionService.request(&"player", &"ch01.concourse_west.turnstile_gate", &"use")` is called
- **THEN** the result has `ok == false` and `reason == &"jammed"`, and `passage_blocked` remains `true`.

### `TEST-PZ001-02` — Unsupported ability on static pillar returns `&"unsupported"`
- **GIVEN** `CH1-PZ-001` at `tick = 0`
- **WHEN** `InteractionService.request(&"player", &"ch01.concourse_west.support_pillar_01", &"rewind")` is called
- **THEN** the result has `ok == false` and `reason == &"unsupported"`.

### `TEST-PZ001-03` — Rewinding turnstile restores pre-arrival `initial_history` (`open`)
- **GIVEN** `CH1-PZ-001` at `tick = 10` where `ch01.concourse_west.turnstile_gate` has `initial_history = [{"arm_state": &"open", "passage_blocked": false}]`
- **WHEN** `InteractionService.request(&"player", &"ch01.concourse_west.turnstile_gate", &"rewind")` is called
- **THEN** the result has `ok == true`, `turnstile_gate.capture_state()` equals `{"arm_state": &"open", "passage_blocked": false}`, and `interaction_applied` is emitted at `tick = 10`.

### `TEST-PZ001-04` — Rewinding an already-open turnstile at oldest history entry returns `&"no_history"`
- **GIVEN** `ch01.concourse_west.turnstile_gate` has already been rewound to `arm_state == &"open"` (cursor at `tick -1`)
- **WHEN** `InteractionService.request(&"player", &"ch01.concourse_west.turnstile_gate", &"rewind")` is called a second time
- **THEN** the result has `ok == false` and `reason == &"no_history"`, and `arm_state` remains `&"open"`.

### `TEST-PZ001-05` — Crossing exit trigger marks `CH1-PZ-001` solved
- **GIVEN** `ch01.concourse_west.turnstile_gate` has `passage_blocked == false`
- **WHEN** `InteractionService.trigger_enter(&"player", &"ch01.concourse_west.exit_trigger_concourse")` is called
- **THEN** `PuzzleService.is_solved(&"CH1-PZ-001")` becomes `true`.
