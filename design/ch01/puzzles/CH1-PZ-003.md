# Puzzle Specification: CH1-PZ-003 — The Delayed Platform

## 1. Standard Puzzle Specification (`PUZZLE_DESIGN.md` §2 & §3)

- **Puzzle ID:** `CH1-PZ-003`
- **Name:** The Delayed Platform
- **Chapter / Room:** Chapter 1 (*The Forgotten Station*) / Security Crossing (`ch01.security_crossing`, Beat 05)
- **Narrative purpose:** Introduce the **Temporal Echo** system (`ADR-008`)—allowing Elias to record and replay a deterministic ghost of his own actions (`&"echo.0"`). Provoke the eerie realization that cooperating with a past version of himself feels unnatural yet familiar to Elias, accompanied by The Operator's PA warning: *"Don't stare at it too long. An echo only does what you already chose to do."*
- **Player objective:** Keep the security gate (`ch01.security_crossing.security_gate_01`) open and the retractable bridge platform (`ch01.security_crossing.bridge_platform_01`) extended by recording an `Echo` of Elias standing on the distant pressure switch (`ch01.security_crossing.pressure_switch_01`), then replaying the `Echo` while Elias walks across.
- **Required abilities:** `Echo` (record, replay, reset)
- **Required objects:**
  - `ch01.security_crossing.echo_recorder_pedestal` (Diegetic Echo calibration pedestal / tutorial anchor that unlocks/initiates Echo mode in `ch01.security_crossing`)
  - `ch01.security_crossing.pressure_switch_01` (Floor weight-sensitive pressure plate, `Area2D` trigger)
  - `ch01.security_crossing.security_gate_01` (Chrono-shielded security barrier guarding the bridge approach)
  - `ch01.security_crossing.bridge_platform_01` (Retractable walkway spanning the transit trench behind the security gate)
  - `ch01.security_crossing.transit_pass_pickup` (Optional progression keycard/document on the security booth desk with `progression = true`, demonstrating `&"echo_forbidden"`)
  - `ch01.security_crossing.safe_anchor_west` (`Marker2D` safe relocation point on the entry side of the bridge)
  - `ch01.security_crossing.reset_anchor_01` (Echo & crossing reset console on the west platform)
  - `ch01.security_crossing.exit_trigger_lounge` (`Area2D` puzzle completion trigger at the Waiting Lounge doorway)
- **Initial room state:**
  - Elias enters the west deck of `ch01.security_crossing`.
  - A deep rail trench separates the west deck from the east doorway to the Waiting Lounge (`ch01.waiting_lounge`).
  - `ch01.security_crossing.security_gate_01` is `gate_state = &"closed"` (`passage_blocked = true`), and `ch01.security_crossing.bridge_platform_01` is `platform_state = &"retracted"` (`walkable = false`).
  - On the south alcove of the west deck (~160 px from the gate) sits `ch01.security_crossing.pressure_switch_01` (`occupied_actors = []`, `is_pressed = false`), wired to the gate and bridge by an unlit floor cable.
  - Both `security_gate_01` and `bridge_platform_01` are housed in copper chrono-shielded casings (`capabilities = NONE`), preventing `Rewind` or `Freeze` cheeses.
- **Rules the player must discover:**
  1. `pressure_switch_01` remains active (`is_pressed == true`) **only** while at least one entity (`&"player"` or `&"echo.0"`) occupies its trigger volume (`occupied_actors.size() > 0`). Stepping off immediately closes `security_gate_01` and retracts `bridge_platform_01` on the same tick.
  2. `security_gate_01` and `bridge_platform_01` are chrono-shielded (`capabilities == NONE`), so `Freeze` and `Rewind` return `&"unsupported"`.
  3. Recording an `Echo` captures timestamped trigger events (`trigger_enter` / `trigger_exit`) and interactions up to `1200` ticks (20.0 s), with max `1` active echo (`&"echo.0"`).
  4. Per `ADR-008`, when Echo playback finishes, `&"echo.0"` **remains at its final recorded position and keeps its trigger occupancy** until the echo is reset or replaced—allowing the ghost to hold `pressure_switch_01` indefinitely while Elias crosses.
  5. Echoes cannot interact with `progression = true` objects (`transit_pass_pickup` rejects `&"echo.0"` with `&"echo_forbidden"`).
- **Step-by-step intended solution:**
  1. Stand near `ch01.security_crossing.pressure_switch_01` (or interact with `echo_recorder_pedestal`) and start `Echo` recording (`rel_tick = 0`).
  2. Walk onto `ch01.security_crossing.pressure_switch_01` (`trigger_enter` captured at `rel_tick = 30..90`). Observe `security_gate_01` open and `bridge_platform_01` extend.
  3. While standing on `pressure_switch_01`, stop `Echo` recording (`rel_tick = 60..180`).
  4. Walk off `pressure_switch_01` (`trigger_exit` for `&"player"`, gate closes) and position Elias near the threshold of `security_gate_01`.
  5. Activate `Echo` playback: `&"echo.0"` spawns, walks onto `pressure_switch_01` at `rel_tick = 30..90`, and remains standing on `pressure_switch_01` after playback finishes (`ADR-008` persistent final occupancy).
  6. With `security_gate_01` held open and `bridge_platform_01` held extended by `&"echo.0"`, walk through the gate, across the platform, and into `ch01.security_crossing.exit_trigger_lounge`.
- **Alternative valid solutions:**
  - **Start Recording Already on the Switch:** If the player stands on `pressure_switch_01` *before* pressing Record, `EchoService` seeds an initial `[rel_tick: 0, kind: &"trigger_enter", target_id: &"ch01.security_crossing.pressure_switch_01", interaction_id: &"trigger_enter", payload: {}]` for currently occupied triggers, or the player steps off and back onto the switch during recording. In both cases, stopping the recording while on the switch holds the gate open during playback.
  - **Timed Mid-Playback Crossing:** If the player records standing on the switch for several seconds (`e.g. 300 ticks`) and then steps off before stopping recording, the gate stays open during the `[enter_tick .. exit_tick]` window, which is also sufficient if Elias is already waiting right beside `security_gate_01`.
- **Failure states:**
  - **Stopping Recording After Stepping Off Switch (and Not Crossing in Time):** If the recorded sequence includes `trigger_exit` at `rel_tick_exit` and Elias hasn't finished crossing before `rel_tick_exit`, the safety railing nudges Elias to `safe_anchor_west` (if on the retracting bridge) and the player can simply replay or re-record.
  - **Attempting Freeze/Rewind on Gate or Switch:** Returns `&"unsupported"` (*"Chrono-shielded security circuit—cannot be frozen or rewound."*).
  - **Attempting Progression Pickup via Echo:** If `&"echo.0"` replays a `&"use"` interaction on `transit_pass_pickup` (`progression = true`), `InteractionService` rejects it with `&"echo_forbidden"` and emits `echo_event_failed`.
- **Reset behavior:**
  - Pressing `echo_reset` (`R` / Joypad Y, `ADR-015`) or interacting with `ch01.security_crossing.reset_anchor_01` clears `&"echo.0"` and dispatches `InteractionService.trigger_exit(&"echo.0", &"ch01.security_crossing.pressure_switch_01")` (`ADR-008`).
  - While `UNSOLVED`, if `&"player"` is standing on `bridge_platform_01` when `pressure_switch_01` loses occupancy (e.g., player resets the echo while standing mid-bridge), `bridge_platform_01` relocates `&"player"` to `ch01.security_crossing.safe_anchor_west` before setting `walkable = false` (`ADR-016`).
  - After `SOLVED` (`ADR-016`), `security_gate_01` and `bridge_platform_01` remain permanently open/extended even if `echo_reset` or `reset_anchor_01` is activated.
- **Hint 1 (nudge):** *"The switch reacts to weight, not identity. Which of the two plates feeds the gate?"*
- **Hint 2 (rule reminder):** *"Something can remain on the switch while Elias moves away. An Echo stays at its final recorded position until you reset it. An Echo cannot take a pass."*
- **Hint 3 (next action):** *"Take the staff pass, record an Echo on the south plate and replay it, cross the bridge, and use the pass at the card reader."*
- **Success feedback:**
  - **Visual:** Floor cable from `pressure_switch_01` to `security_gate_01` illuminates in steady cyan; the security gate bars retract upward (**chevron icon `[>>]`**), and `bridge_platform_01` locks across the trench with dual safety rails raised.
  - **Audio:** Heavy servo motor hum, pneumatic latch lock, and a phase-shifted harmonic chime as `&"echo.0"` holds the plate.
- **Narrative reward:** Grants access to the Waiting Lounge (`ch01.waiting_lounge`) and triggers The Operator's PA line about echoes.
- **Accessibility considerations:**
  - **Color-independent cues:** Floor cable displays an animated **marching-chevron pattern (`>>>`)** when active vs. a **dashed broken line (`- - -`)** when inactive. `pressure_switch_01` shows a **depressed weight glyph `[v]`** when occupied vs. **raised `[A]`** when empty.
  - **Zero reflex requirement in intended solution:** Because `&"echo.0"` retains final-position occupancy (`ADR-008`), once playback reaches the switch, the gate stays open indefinitely with no rush to cross.
- **Acceptance tests:** See §6 below (`TEST-PZ003-01` through `TEST-PZ003-05`).

## 4. Echo Logic Track Specification (`ADR-008`)

- **Echo Limits (`ADR-008`):** Max active echoes = `1` (`&"echo.0"`). Max recording length = `1200` ticks (`20.0 s`).
- **Intended Solution Tick Budget:** **`90` to `180` ticks (`1.5–3.0 s`)** — well inside the `≤ 900` tick design target and `1200` tick cap.
- **Exact Logic Track Event Array Produced by Intended Solution:**

| Event # | `rel_tick` Range | `kind` (`StringName`) | `target_id` (`StringName`) | `interaction_id` (`StringName`) | `payload` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| *(Start)* | `0` | *(record_start)* | — | — | `{}` |
| **1** | `30 .. 90` | `&"trigger_enter"` | `&"ch01.security_crossing.pressure_switch_01"` | `&"trigger_enter"` | `{}` |
| *(Stop)* | `60 .. 180` | *(record_stop)* | — | — | `{}` |

- **Post-Playback Occupancy Behavior (`ADR-008`):**
  - Because no `trigger_exit` for `pressure_switch_01` occurs before `record_stop`, when playback reaches `rel_tick_end` (`60..180`), `&"echo.0"` remains at its final recorded position inside `pressure_switch_01` and **retains its `occupied_actors` entry** until the echo is reset, replaced, or deleted.

## 5. Soft-Lock & Reachable Bad-State Analysis

| Reachable State | Potential Issue | Recovery Path (Zero Save Reload Required) |
|---|---|---|
| Player resets or replaces `&"echo.0"` while standing midway across `bridge_platform_01` | Could the retracting bridge strand or drop Elias into the rail trench? | **No.** When `pressure_switch_01.is_pressed` transitions to `false` while `bridge_platform_01.player_on_bridge == true`, the platform safety interlock moves `player` to `ch01.security_crossing.safe_anchor_west` before setting `walkable = false`. |
| Player records 1200 ticks (20 s) of wandering without stepping on `pressure_switch_01` | Does hitting the 1200-tick cap break the recorder? | **No.** At `rel_tick == 1200`, `EchoService` automatically stops recording and notifies the HUD; the player can press Record again (replacing the recording) or Reset at any time. |
| Player crosses to the east side (`exit_trigger_lounge`) and then resets the Echo | Could resetting the Echo after crossing trap the player on the east side? | **No.** Entering `exit_trigger_lounge` marks `CH1-PZ-003` as `SOLVED` in `PuzzleService`, which engages the mechanical bridge lock on the east side so `security_gate_01` and `bridge_platform_01` remain permanently passable regardless of future Echo resets. |

## 6. Acceptance Tests (`GIVEN / WHEN / THEN`)

### `TEST-PZ003-01` — Stepping onto and off `pressure_switch_01` toggles gate and bridge on the exact tick
- **GIVEN** `CH1-PZ-003` at `tick = 0` with `security_gate_01.passage_blocked == true` and `bridge_platform_01.walkable == false`
- **WHEN** `InteractionService.trigger_enter(&"player", &"ch01.security_crossing.pressure_switch_01")` is called at `tick = 10`
- **THEN** `pressure_switch_01.is_pressed == true`, `security_gate_01.passage_blocked == false`, and `bridge_platform_01.walkable == true`; and when `InteractionService.trigger_exit(&"player", &"ch01.security_crossing.pressure_switch_01")` is called at `tick = 20`, `is_pressed == false`, `passage_blocked == true`, and `walkable == false`.

### `TEST-PZ003-02` — Echo playback holds `pressure_switch_01` open after playback completes (`ADR-008`)
- **GIVEN** an Echo recording started at `tick = 0`, captured `trigger_enter(&"player", &"ch01.security_crossing.pressure_switch_01")` at `rel_tick = 40`, and stopped at `rel_tick = 80`, after which `&"player"` exited the switch at `tick = 90` (`is_pressed == false`)
- **WHEN** `EchoService.start_playback()` is called at `tick = 100` and `SimClock.step(200)` advances to `tick = 300` (well past `rel_tick_end = 80`)
- **THEN** at `tick = 140` (`100 + 40`), `&"echo.0"` enters `pressure_switch_01`, and at `tick = 300`, `pressure_switch_01.occupied_actors` still contains `&"echo.0"`, `security_gate_01.passage_blocked == false`, and `bridge_platform_01.walkable == true`.

### `TEST-PZ003-03` — Resetting the Echo dispatches `trigger_exit` and safely relocates player if on bridge
- **GIVEN** `&"echo.0"` is holding `pressure_switch_01` (`is_pressed == true`) and `&"player"` is standing on `bridge_platform_01` (`player_on_bridge == true`)
- **WHEN** `EchoService.reset_echo()` (or `InteractionService.request(&"player", &"ch01.security_crossing.reset_anchor_01", &"reset")`) is called
- **THEN** `trigger_exit(&"echo.0", &"ch01.security_crossing.pressure_switch_01")` is dispatched, `player.global_position` is moved to `safe_anchor_west.global_position`, and `bridge_platform_01.walkable` becomes `false`.

### `TEST-PZ003-04` — Echo attempting to collect `progression = true` pickup is rejected with `&"echo_forbidden"`
- **GIVEN** `ch01.security_crossing.transit_pass_pickup` has `progression == true` and `collected == false`
- **WHEN** `InteractionService.request(&"echo.0", &"ch01.security_crossing.transit_pass_pickup", &"use")` is called during Echo playback
- **THEN** the result has `ok == false` and `reason == &"echo_forbidden"`, `transit_pass_pickup.collected` remains `false`, and `EchoService` emits `echo_event_failed`.

### `TEST-PZ003-05` — Freeze and Rewind on chrono-shielded security gate return `&"unsupported"`
- **GIVEN** `CH1-PZ-003` at `tick = 0`
- **WHEN** `InteractionService.request(&"player", &"ch01.security_crossing.security_gate_01", &"freeze")` and `InteractionService.request(&"player", &"ch01.security_crossing.security_gate_01", &"rewind")` are called
- **THEN** both requests return `ok == false` and `reason == &"unsupported"`.
