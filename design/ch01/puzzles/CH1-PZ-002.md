# Puzzle Specification: CH1-PZ-002 — The Runaway Shutter

## 1. Standard Puzzle Specification (`PUZZLE_DESIGN.md` §2)

- **Puzzle ID:** `CH1-PZ-002`
- **Name:** The Runaway Shutter
- **Chapter / Room:** Chapter 1 (*The Forgotten Station*) / Maintenance Bay (`ch01.maintenance_bay`, Beat 04)
- **Narrative purpose:** Teach `Freeze` and demonstrate how combining two pocket-watch abilities (`Rewind` + `Freeze`) across coupled industrial mechanisms lets Elias stabilize a hazardous transit service corridor. Reinforce that ELYSIUM's infrastructure was suffering chronometric wear before the `23:59` lock.
- **Player objective:** `Rewind` the dropped fire shutter (`ch01.maintenance_bay.fire_shutter`) to its raised pre-arrival position, then `Freeze` its stripped counterweight gear (`ch01.maintenance_bay.counterweight_gear`) before the shutter slides shut so Elias can walk safely underneath.
- **Required abilities:** `Rewind`, `Freeze` (optional `Trace` on the maintenance log/gear housing)
- **Required objects:**
  - `ch01.maintenance_bay.fire_shutter` (Heavy vertical fire shutter with `REWIND` capability)
  - `ch01.maintenance_bay.counterweight_gear` (Exposed wall-mounted gear assembly with `FREEZE` capability)
  - `ch01.maintenance_bay.shutter_threshold_trigger` (`Area2D` footprint under the shutter for non-lethal safety clutch)
  - `ch01.maintenance_bay.safe_anchor_west` (`Marker2D` safe knockback/relocation point on entry side)
  - `ch01.maintenance_bay.safe_anchor_east` (`Marker2D` safe relocation point on exit side)
  - `ch01.maintenance_bay.reset_anchor_01` (Pneumatic release valve / puzzle reset anchor reachable from west side)
  - `ch01.maintenance_bay.exit_trigger_east` (`Area2D` completion trigger on the east side of the shutter)
- **Initial room state:**
  - `ch01.maintenance_bay.fire_shutter` is in `shutter_state = &"closed"`, `hold_ticks = 0`, `passage_blocked = true`, sealing the corridor to the Security Crossing.
  - `ch01.maintenance_bay.counterweight_gear` is mounted on the north wall beside the shutter in `gear_state = &"idle"`, `is_frozen = false`. Its stripped teeth glint with a blue-white temporal fracture aura indicating `FREEZE` support.
- **Rules the player must discover:**
  1. `Rewind` on `fire_shutter` restores its pre-arrival `"raised"` state (`hold_ticks = 180` = 3.0 seconds at 60 Hz), clearing the doorway (`passage_blocked = false`).
  2. Because the counterweight gear's teeth are stripped, `hold_ticks` counts down by 1 per physics tick while `counterweight_gear.is_frozen == false`. After `180` ticks (3.0 s)—too fast for Elias to walk all the way through from the casting spot—the shutter slides back to `"closed"`.
  3. `fire_shutter` supports `REWIND` only, while `counterweight_gear` supports `FREEZE` only (targeting the wrong ability returns `&"unsupported"` with a specific hint message).
  4. Freezing `counterweight_gear` *before* rewinding `fire_shutter` mechanically locks the spindle (`fire_shutter` returns `&"frozen"` when `Rewind` is attempted while `counterweight_gear.is_frozen == true`). The player must **first `Rewind` the shutter, then `Freeze` the gear** while `shutter_state == &"raised"`.
- **Step-by-step intended solution:**
  1. Select `Rewind` on the pocket watch and activate `&"rewind"` on `ch01.maintenance_bay.fire_shutter`.
  2. `fire_shutter` rises to `shutter_state = &"raised"` (`hold_ticks = 180`, `passage_blocked = false`), and `counterweight_gear` enters `gear_state = &"slipping"` (ticking down audibly and visually over 3.0 seconds).
  3. Within the 180-tick (3.0 s) window, switch the pocket watch to `Freeze` and activate `&"freeze"` on `ch01.maintenance_bay.counterweight_gear`.
  4. `counterweight_gear.is_frozen` becomes `true` (`gear_state = &"locked"`), halting the `hold_ticks` countdown and locking `fire_shutter` in `"raised"` indefinitely.
  5. Walk underneath `fire_shutter` into `ch01.maintenance_bay.exit_trigger_east` to complete the puzzle.
- **Alternative valid solutions:**
  - **Last-Tick Freeze:** Any `Freeze` activation on `counterweight_gear` while `hold_ticks >= 1` (`1..180` ticks remaining) locks `fire_shutter` in `"raised"` (`passage_blocked = false`) and allows passage.
- **Failure states:**
  - **Timer Expiry (Non-Lethal):** If `180` ticks elapse after `Rewind` without freezing `counterweight_gear`, `fire_shutter` slides back to `shutter_state = &"closed"`. If Elias is inside `shutter_threshold_trigger` when `hold_ticks` hits `0`, the safety clutch gently pushes Elias back to `safe_anchor_west` with zero damage and zero soft-lock.
  - **Wrong Order (`Freeze` before `Rewind`):** If the player freezes `counterweight_gear` while `fire_shutter` is `"closed"` and then tries to `Rewind` `fire_shutter`, `InteractionService` returns `&"frozen"` (*"The counterweight spindle is frozen solid—unfreeze the gear before rewinding the shutter."*).
  - **Wrong Target Ability:** Using `Freeze` on `fire_shutter` or `Rewind` on `counterweight_gear` returns `&"unsupported"` with guidance.
- **Reset behavior:**
  - Interacting (`&"reset"` / `&"use"`) with `ch01.maintenance_bay.reset_anchor_01` unfreezes `counterweight_gear` (`is_frozen = false`, `gear_state = &"idle"`), sets `fire_shutter` to tick-0 `closed` (`hold_ticks = 0`, `passage_blocked = true`), and re-seeds `fire_shutter.initial_history` (`raised`, `hold_ticks = 180`).
  - Furthermore, whenever `fire_shutter` naturally slips from `raised` back to `closed` at `hold_ticks == 0`, its `raised` state remains in its ring buffer so the player can immediately `Rewind` it again without needing to press the reset anchor.
- **Hint 1 (nudge):** *"Rewinding the shutter lifts it, but something on the wall is letting it slip back down."*
- **Hint 2 (rule reminder):** *"The shutter and its counterweight gear respond to different watch abilities. Lift the shutter first, then stop the gear from turning."*
- **Hint 3 (next action):** *"Use Rewind on `ch01.maintenance_bay.fire_shutter`, then switch to Freeze and target `ch01.maintenance_bay.counterweight_gear` while the shutter is up."*
- **Success feedback:**
  - **Visual:** Crystalline frost-blue stasis rings lock around the spinning teeth of `counterweight_gear`; the shutter's countdown bar halts and turns into a **padlock icon `[LOCK]`**, and the floor guide strip illuminates toward the east exit.
  - **Audio:** Rapid gear ratcheting abruptly crystallizes into a resonant glass-bell tone as the shutter locks overhead.
- **Narrative reward:** Opens the route to the Security Crossing (`ch01.security_crossing`) and allows inspection of the technician's clipboard (`ch01.maintenance_bay.doc_tech_log`) detailing pre-incident chronometric backflow.
- **Accessibility considerations:**
  - **Generous timing window:** `180` ticks (3.0 full seconds) to switch ability and target the adjacent gear; if Reduced Effects / Accessibility Assist is enabled in `Settings`, the hold window extends to `300` ticks (5.0 s).
  - **Color-independent indicators:** `counterweight_gear` displays a **spinning gear icon `(o)`** when slipping, a **hexagonal snowflake/lock icon `[*]`** when frozen, and a radial segmented dial (6 wedges, 30 ticks per wedge) showing remaining `hold_ticks` without relying on color alone.
  - **Non-lethal guarantee:** The shutter never deals damage or kills the player.
- **Acceptance tests:** See §5 below (`TEST-PZ002-01` through `TEST-PZ002-05`).

## 4. Soft-Lock & Reachable Bad-State Analysis

| Reachable State | Potential Issue | Recovery Path (Zero Save Reload Required) |
|---|---|---|
| Player rewinds `fire_shutter` and lets `180` ticks expire without freezing `counterweight_gear` | Does `fire_shutter` run out of `initial_history` after slipping shut? | **No.** When `hold_ticks` reaches `0`, transitioning from `raised` → `closed` appends `closed` after `raised` in the ring buffer (per `ADR-007`), so activating `&"rewind"` again immediately restores `raised` (`hold_ticks = 180`). |
| Player freezes `counterweight_gear` while `fire_shutter` is still `"closed"` | Can the player get stuck unable to rewind the shutter? | **No.** Attempting `&"rewind"` on `fire_shutter` returns `&"frozen"` with explicit UI guidance; activating `&"freeze"` on `counterweight_gear` again toggles `is_frozen = false` (or using `reset_anchor_01` clears it). |
| Player stands directly underneath `fire_shutter` (`shutter_threshold_trigger`) when `hold_ticks` reaches `0` (or unfreezes the gear while standing underneath) | Could the closing shutter crush or trap the player inside its collision box? | **No.** When `shutter_state` transitions to `&"closed"` while `shutter_threshold_trigger.player_inside == true`, the safety clutch moves `player` to `ch01.maintenance_bay.safe_anchor_west` before enabling `passage_blocked = true`. |
| Player crosses to the east side and then unfreezes `counterweight_gear` from the east side | Could the player lock themselves on the east side unable to return west? | **No.** Once `exit_trigger_east` is entered (`CH1-PZ-002` `SOLVED`), both `fire_shutter` and `counterweight_gear` remain targetable from either side of the grate, so the player can always `Rewind` + `Freeze` again if backtracking. |

## 5. Acceptance Tests (`GIVEN / WHEN / THEN`)

### `TEST-PZ002-01` — Rewinding shutter raises it for 180 ticks, then slips shut if gear is not frozen
- **GIVEN** `CH1-PZ-002` at `tick = 0` with `fire_shutter.shutter_state == &"closed"` and `counterweight_gear.is_frozen == false`
- **WHEN** `InteractionService.request(&"player", &"ch01.maintenance_bay.fire_shutter", &"rewind")` is called and `SimClock.step(179)` is executed
- **THEN** at `tick = 179`, `fire_shutter.shutter_state == &"raised"` and `hold_ticks == 1`; and after `SimClock.step(1)` (`tick = 180`), `fire_shutter.shutter_state == &"closed"` and `passage_blocked == true`.

### `TEST-PZ002-02` — Freezing counterweight gear while shutter is raised halts slip countdown indefinitely
- **GIVEN** `fire_shutter` was rewound to `&"raised"` at `tick = 0` and `SimClock.step(60)` advanced to `tick = 60` (`hold_ticks == 120`)
- **WHEN** `InteractionService.request(&"player", &"ch01.maintenance_bay.counterweight_gear", &"freeze")` is called and `SimClock.step(300)` advances to `tick = 360`
- **THEN** `counterweight_gear.is_frozen == true`, `fire_shutter.shutter_state == &"raised"`, `fire_shutter.hold_ticks == 120`, and `fire_shutter.passage_blocked == false`.

### `TEST-PZ002-03` — Freezing gear while shutter is closed prevents shutter rewind with `&"frozen"`
- **GIVEN** `CH1-PZ-002` at `tick = 0` with `fire_shutter.shutter_state == &"closed"`
- **WHEN** `InteractionService.request(&"player", &"ch01.maintenance_bay.counterweight_gear", &"freeze")` is called (`is_frozen = true`), followed by `InteractionService.request(&"player", &"ch01.maintenance_bay.fire_shutter", &"rewind")`
- **THEN** the rewind request returns `ok == false` and `reason == &"frozen"`, and `fire_shutter.shutter_state` remains `&"closed"`.

### `TEST-PZ002-04` — Safety clutch relocates player to `safe_anchor_west` if shutter closes while player is in threshold
- **GIVEN** `fire_shutter` is `&"raised"` (`hold_ticks == 5`), `counterweight_gear.is_frozen == false`, and `InteractionService.trigger_enter(&"player", &"ch01.maintenance_bay.shutter_threshold_trigger")` has occurred
- **WHEN** `SimClock.step(5)` expires `hold_ticks` to `0`
- **THEN** `player.global_position` equals `safe_anchor_west.global_position`, `shutter_threshold_trigger.player_inside == false`, and `fire_shutter.passage_blocked == true` without trapping the player.

### `TEST-PZ002-05` — Shutter can be rewound again after slipping closed without manual reset
- **GIVEN** `fire_shutter` was rewound at `tick = 0` and allowed to slip back to `&"closed"` at `tick = 180`
- **WHEN** `InteractionService.request(&"player", &"ch01.maintenance_bay.fire_shutter", &"rewind")` is called at `tick = 190`
- **THEN** the result has `ok == true`, `fire_shutter.shutter_state == &"raised"`, and `fire_shutter.hold_ticks == 180`.
